Server Rules

Magical Decree of Conduct

Keep the castle fun for everyone. Breaking these may land you in detention — or worse.

I

General Conduct

4 rules
  • Treat all students and staff with respect.
  • No harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
  • Keep chat family-friendly and appropriate.
  • English is the primary language in all channels.
II

Roleplay Guidelines

4 rules
  • Stay in character during roleplay sessions.
  • No powergaming or godmodding.
  • Respect the established magical lore.
  • Follow staff instructions during events.
III

Technical Rules

4 rules
  • No exploits or unauthorized mods.
  • Report bugs to staff immediately.
  • No alternate accounts allowed.
  • Keep your wand to yourself.

Questions about a rule? Send an Owl Post to management.
