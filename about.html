Our Story

Built by players, for players

Founded in 2023, Magic School X has grown into a thriving community of witches and wizards from around the world, delivering an immersive Hogwarts experience in Garry's Mod.

We started this server because we were tired of pay-to-play servers and power-tripping staff. Here, freedom and fun come first.
